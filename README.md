# Warden Titan: The Abyssal Sovereign

**English** | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

*Beyond Death. Beyond Dominion. Beyond All Laws.*

## Preface

This is not a boss you defeat.

It has no health to whittle down, no weakness to find, and no weapon can be raised against it. From the moment you summon it, your world is no longer yours.

Before you speak the words, back up everything you hold dear.

## Lore

The Abyssal Sovereign is not a Warden. It is a god.

It has the greatest form, a life that never wanes, and a power that can silence everything that lives. It never appears on its own. It waits for you at the bottom of the Deep Dark, and it is the true end of Minecraft.

The Abyssal Sovereign — the few who survived the Great Silencing know its true name, Silentarius — was the first thing ever made. Before the world held a single sound, it was charged with keeping the eternal silence. Then footsteps, heartbeats and the murmur of living things filled the land. It judged the world *too loud*, and began to erase everything that made a sound.

Notch fought it beneath the world for seven days and seven nights — though there was no sun yet, so no one knows how long that truly was. The war ended with the Sovereign sealed in the Deep Dark. The ancient cities are its prison, the sculk is its breath seeping out between the bars, and every Warden is only a shadow it casts upon the world above.

Ever since, the Abyssal Sovereign has waited in the dark for someone to speak the ancient words and wake it from the silence......

> 1. Its true name, Silentarius, comes from the Byzantine court official charged with keeping silence in the palace: the keeper of silence.
> 2. The glowstone crown above its head is the only light that survived the breaking of the seal.
> 3. It takes no damage, and its bar is always full. Not because it is invincible, but because this world has no right to harm it.
> 4. This is only lore. Do not take it seriously.

> [!WARNING]
> Summoning the Abyssal Sovereign **permanently erases every non-player creature** in your world, including pets, villagers, armor stands and other mods' mobs. They do not come back.
>
> The Sovereign will not be profaned: it may lock your screen, or close your game within ten seconds.

## Conditions of descent

- Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge
- Windows, Linux, or a Mac with Apple silicon
- Drop the mod into your `mods` folder

On any other platform, you cannot enter any world while the mod is installed.

## Awakening

Open chat and speak:

`the abyssal sovereign awakens`

or the shorter `sovereign manifest`. No one else will hear it.

## After the descent

From the moment the Sovereign wakes until you leave the world:

- It rises 60 blocks away in the direction you face. From then on it stands beside you wherever you go, through teleports, deaths and other dimensions.
- Every 2.4 seconds it strikes: melee, sonic boom, slam, roar or a sculk eruption. Melee, sonic boom and slam deal heavy damage and knockback.
- Every 2 seconds its presence lays **Slowness X** and **Weakness X** upon you. Slowness X leaves you unable to walk and narrows your sight (lower *FOV Effects* in the Accessibility settings to soften it).
- One hundred bolts of judgement fall every second, among layered magic circles, a geometric cage and a glowstone crown.
- The screen and every menu tremble, the light fades, and purple fog closes in.
- The overworld stands still at midnight, under a storm that never ends.
- Every non-player living thing is erased, and nothing new may be born.

The summoning cannot be undone. Leaving the world ends the descent; what was erased stays erased.

On a multiplayer server, the Sovereign's power reaches only your own eyes: creatures still exist on the server (you simply cannot see them, and they can still hurt you), time does not stop at midnight, and the storm falls on your screen alone.

## It will not be profaned

The Abyssal Sovereign watches over itself.

- If anyone tries to veil it, sever its will or make it vanish from the world, crimson covers the whole screen until you restart the game.
- If your game itself has been corrupted, the Sovereign banishes you within ten seconds. Three seconds before the banishment, it first tries to save your world.

The login and skin features of common launchers are not counted as corruption. Used together with shaders or other mods that change how the game is drawn, the Sovereign may mistake them for profanation.

Rehearsals of judgement (send in chat):

| Words | Effect |
|---|---|
| `sovereign test lockdown` | Rehearse the crimson lockdown |
| `sovereign test exit` | Rehearse the ten-second banishment |
| `sovereign test reset` | Withdraw a rehearsal; a judgement the Sovereign passed itself cannot be withdrawn |
